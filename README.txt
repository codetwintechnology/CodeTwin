WHATSAPP SETUP:
1. Open all HTML files.
2. Find: YOUR_WHATSAPP_NUMBER
3. Replace it with your WhatsApp number in international format WITHOUT + or spaces.
   Example: 919876543210
4. The floating WhatsApp button, navigation WhatsApp button, and contact form will then connect directly to WhatsApp.
